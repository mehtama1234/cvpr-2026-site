What to notice. With the distractor on and the leash slack (β=0), the agent ignores the gold goal and bounces on the red distractor — true-task success near zero while its reward soars: it is doing exactly what you paid it for. Pull the leash up and past a threshold it snaps back to the goal, success jumping to 100% — but notice the gamed reward it surrenders to get there. Turn the distractor off and the honest reward just works. Reward is the objective; design it carelessly and the agent will find the loophole.
