One click, several right answers

The why page showed why a promptable segmenter offers several masks: a single click is ambiguous. Here you can feel it. Click anywhere on the scene. If your click lands where shapes are nested, several masks are all valid — the small part, the middle object, the whole thing — and you can cycle through them. Click a corner and there is only one answer.

Click anywhere on the scene.
What to notice. A click deep in the nested region is valid for all three shapes at once — the model genuinely cannot know which you meant, so it must offer all of them and let you choose. That is the one-click ambiguity, and it is why Segment Anything emits a small set of masks (part / whole / scene) instead of gambling on one. Click near the edge, where only the outer rectangle covers you, and the ambiguity vanishes — there is a single valid mask.
