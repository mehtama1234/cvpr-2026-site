accuracy
What to notice. At 1 sample every method sits at the model's weak one-shot accuracy. Turn the dial up and voting climbs steadily (the correct answer out-votes the scattered wrong ones); the perfect verifier rockets to 100% (one good sample in the batch is enough); the noisy verifier lags the whole way — it keeps selecting confident-but-wrong answers. Same compute, very different ceilings: the aggregation, not the raw sampling, decides how far extra thinking gets you.
