Learning from reward — RL across CVPR & Robotics

The why page argued it from first principles: no labels, only a reward — a policy discovers good behaviour by trial and error, and the peril is a reward it can game. This page shows the two worlds RL now lives in: aligning image, video, and reasoning models with a reward at CVPR, and learning control on real hardware in robotics.

What changes between vision and a robot

axisCVPR 2026 (vision)Robotics 2025
What the reward isa preference / quality score for a generated image, video, or answertask success — did it walk, grasp, reach the goal
The signature methodGRPO / DPO on top of a pretrained modelpolicy gradient / actor-critic, often teacher-student sim-to-real
The dangerreward hacking — gaming the proxy scorereward hacking + unsafe exploration on real hardware
The leashKL to the pretrained referencesafety constraints, curricula, sim before real
The through-line. Reinforcement learning optimizes a reward, not a label — a policy learns by trial and error what earns reward, and its recurring peril is a reward it can game. In vision RL aligns generators and reasoners with a preference score; in robotics it is the native way to learn control from task-success reward — the same primitive, a score to satisfy versus a task to accomplish.
