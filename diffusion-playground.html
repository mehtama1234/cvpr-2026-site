Press Denoise: the noise cloud walks onto the target modes. Then raise Guidance with unequal density and watch the small modes empty out.
rings = target modes (size ∝ density) · dots = samples, coloured by the mode they land on · grey dots are still travelling
What to notice. (1) With few steps the dots never reach the modes — most of the quality is in the first handful of steps, exactly the why-page's steps curve. (2) With unequal density, push guidance up: the sampler abandons the small modes and piles onto the big ones — that is mode collapse, the price of fidelity. (3) Flip density to equal and the same high guidance no longer collapses anything — proof that collapse needs unequal modes to feed on, the exact thing the real trained run taught us.
