Plan by dreaming

The why page showed an agent that reaches a goal by imagining many short futures in its world model and taking the first step of the best one. Here it runs live. The one knob is how accurate the imagination is — turn up model error and watch the dreamed rollouts scatter and the planning fall apart, just as compounding drift eats a real world model.

← All themesThe big pictureIn the wildThe papers
What to notice. At zero model error the imagined rollouts (faint teal) bundle tightly and the agent drives almost straight to the goal — planning in a perfect model is nearly perfect. Raise the error and each imagined step adds noise that compounds down the rollout, so the fan of dreams sprays wider, the best-looking dream is often a lie, and the agent wanders, overshoots, or stalls. A world model is only worth planning in as far as it can imagine before it drifts.
