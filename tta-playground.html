How hard should it adapt?

The why page showed adaptation recover accuracy for free — then wreck it if pushed too far. Here is that whole trade-off as one dial, walking along the real measured curve. Turn it up from off, and watch accuracy climb off the collapsed floor, crest, and then fall off a cliff.

accuracy
What to notice. At zero you sit at the shifted accuracy — the model frozen while the world moved. A little adaptation (recalibrate, a few steps) is the sweet spot, recovering a big share of the gap toward the clean line with no labels. But keep turning and every extra step makes the model more confident on data it cannot check, until it drifts off a cliff below where it began. Powerful and unstable in the same motion — the craft is stopping at the crest.
