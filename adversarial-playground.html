What to notice. Start at ε=0 — the model is right and confident. Now drag ε up a little. At a budget so small the image looks unchanged to you, the label flips to the wrong class and the model is confident about it. Try all three images: some flip at the slightest touch, some hold out longer — the boundary is thinner in some places than others, but it is thin everywhere. This is one small, undefended model; the same trick works on the largest ones.
