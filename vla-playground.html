What to notice. With the regression head, every Drive ends the same way — a curve straight into the obstacle, because the policy learned the average of the left and right demonstrations, and that average points at the wall. With the generative head, each Drive commits to one side and reaches the target — and pressing Drive again may take the other side, because it samples from the two good options instead of averaging them. That single difference — sample, don't average — is why modern vision-language-action models put a diffusion or token head on top, never a plain regressor.
