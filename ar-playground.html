detail — low (bland) → high (noisy) · the black line is real images
What to notice. At low temperature the model plays it safe and its samples collapse toward a bland, repetitive average — very little detail. At high temperature it gets reckless and the picture dissolves toward noise — more detail than a real image, but the wrong kind. The sweet spot sits near the black line, where the samples have about as much structure as real garments. Boldness buys variety and costs coherence; there is no free setting.
