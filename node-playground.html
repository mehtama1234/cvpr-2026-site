Watch depth become time

The why page says a neural ODE replaces a stack of layers with a smooth flow: the data drifts continuously from time 0 to time 1 until the classes separate. Here is that exact flow, recorded from the real run. Slide time and watch two tangled moons pull apart — not in discrete jumps, but as one continuous deformation of the plane.

What to notice. At t=0 the two classes (teal and gold) are interleaved — no straight line could separate them. As you advance time, the learned velocity field carries every point along a smooth path, bending the whole plane until, by t=1, the two crescents have slid apart into linearly separable blobs. There are no layers here — just a continuous flow, sampled at whatever time-resolution you like. That is what makes depth continuous.
