1 · Energy-based generation and composition

Energy-based models (EBMs) treat probability distributions as unnormalized energy landscapes: low-energy states are high-probability, high-energy states are forbidden. The key insight is additive composition—combine behaviors by summing energy functions—and sampling via energy descent. This section surveys four papers
Mathematical move

Quantity. In 1 · Energy-based generation and composition, the recurring quantity is the energy ranking over possible states.

Rule. The mathematical rule is to assign low energy to valid explanations and high energy to invalid ones, then search or sample from the low-energy region.

Why this use exists. The uses differ by what counts as a good state: image, label, trajectory, edit, or physical plan.

CVPR'26Towards Decompositional Human Motion Generation with Energy-Based Diffusion Models — Models motion as composition of energy-based primitives: each learned energy function E_i scores a motion primitive, and total motion probability is exp(−sum E_i).
CVPR'26EnergyAction: Unimanual to Bimanual Composition with Energy-Based Models — Models each arm policy as an EBM: E_left(s,a) and E_right(s,a).
CVPR'26FrankenMotion: Part-level Human Motion Generation and Composition — Combines diffusion-based generation with hierarchical energy-based conditioning on part, action, and sequence levels.
CVPR'26From Attraction to Equilibrium: Physics-Inspired Semantic Gravitons for Zero-Shot Anomaly Detection — Semantic Graviton Network replaces heuristic fusion with learnable gravitons as dynamic mediators.

What changes between vision and a robot

axisCVPR 2026 (vision)Robotics 2025
What the energy isa learned density landscape to sample or scorea cost surface to plan and act on
The generative formscore-based diffusion / distribution matchingenergy-guided or constraint-guided flow policies
The detection formhigh energy = out-of-distribution / anomalyhigh energy = infeasible / unsafe state
The standing costMCMC mixing (tamed by the diffusion reformulation)shaping the energy so its minima are the goals
The through-line. An energy model describes a distribution by a landscape and needs only its gradient, so the normalizer never appears; the score is that gradient. In vision it generates (as diffusion), composes constraints, and flags anomalies by high energy; in robotics an energy is a cost surface a policy descends — the same primitive, a density to sample versus a cost to minimize. Diffusion is its most successful child.
