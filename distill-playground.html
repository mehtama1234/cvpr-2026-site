What a hard label throws away

The why page says the student learns more from the teacher's soft answer than from a plain label. Here is that soft answer, for real. A hard label would give the student a single 1 and nine 0s. The teacher gives a whole distribution — and the temperature knob controls how much of its hidden 'dark knowledge' shows through.

What to notice. Switch to hard label and the student is told only the winner — every other class is exactly zero, as if a shirt were nothing at all like a t-shirt. Switch back to soft label and the teacher's real opinion appears: the true class still leads, but the near-misses light up. Raise the temperature and those runner-ups grow — the dark knowledge the teacher is quietly handing the student. Turn it too high and everything flattens toward a useless smear. Somewhere in between is where distillation lives.
