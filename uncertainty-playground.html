When many models disagree

The why page showed that a single model's confidence stays high even on inputs it has never seen — a useless alarm — while the disagreement of an ensemble is an honest one. Here you can drive it. Slide the query point out of the known data and watch the single model stay smugly confident while the ensemble's votes scatter.

What to notice. Inside a cluster the seven models all vote the same class and the single model is confident — everyone agrees, correctly. Drag outward and the single model's confidence barely budges (it just picks the nearest known class, sure as ever) — but the ensemble's votes split apart, because out where there was no training data each model guesses differently. Confidence lies; disagreement tells the truth. When agreement collapses, that is the moment to flag the input as unfamiliar.
