Knowledge distillation · how the field actually transfers knowledge now

Teacher into student — distillation across CVPR & Robotics

The why page argued it from first principles: train a small student to copy a big teacher's soft answers — the dark knowledge a hard label throws away — and let the teacher label unlimited unlabelled data. This page shows the many shapes that idea takes, from compressing a fifty-step generator into one step to a privileged sim teacher training an onboard robot.

What changes between vision and a robot

axisCVPR 2026 (vision)Robotics 2025
Who is the teachera bigger network, or a slow many-step generatora simulator that sees privileged truth, or a foundation model too heavy to run
What the student gainsspeed, size, or robustness at near-teacher qualitya policy it can actually run onboard from real sensors
The classic movesoft-label matching, one-step generator distillationprivileged-to-onboard, cross-modal distillation
Why it mattersship frontier quality cheaplythe only way sim-trained skill survives contact with the real robot
The through-line. Distillation moves knowledge from a capable source into a leaner target by matching soft outputs, not hard labels. In vision that compresses slow generators and whole datasets; in robotics it pours a privileged simulator's or foundation model's skill into something a robot can actually run — the same primitive, teacher to student.
