Decision math

Artifact Rehydration Queue is not just a screen of labels. It is a measurement problem: the page is trying to decide whether a claim about the conference system should be trusted under pressure. The page summary says: Local cached artifacts rehydrated from the promoted Colab evidence store for the eight deep viewers.

Hidden quantity

The quantity underneath this page is support: how much the available record should change belief about the system, compared with belief before seeing the record.

Evidence

A receipt, replay, artifact, or linked source is evidence only if it could have looked different when the claim was false. Evidence gets stronger when it is specific, reproducible, and tied to a failure mode.

Decision rule

Update belief by comparing two explanations: the system works as claimed, or the system only appears to work because the evidence is incomplete. Good evidence makes the second explanation expensive.

Failure test

The evidence layer breaks when provenance is unclear, when records cannot be replayed, or when the page reports conclusions without exposing the observations that forced them.

Fundamental principles at play: Bayes · information theory · mcmc. These are the same mathematical ideas used in the math pages, but here they are attached to operational decisions: what is measured, how uncertain the measurement is, and what action the evidence permits.
