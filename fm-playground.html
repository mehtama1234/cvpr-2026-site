What to notice. At 1 step the diffusion samples are a shapeless smear — it cannot cross its curved noise-to-data path in a single jump — while the flow samples already sit on the ring of targets. Give diffusion more steps and it slowly recovers; the flow model was basically there from the start. Same target, same size network, same kind of sampler — the only difference is that flow matching walks a straight path it can cross in a few big steps. That is the whole reason it became the default fast generator.
