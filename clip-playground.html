Query the space by word (open-vocabulary retrieval):
What to notice. At zero alignment the class names huddle in a corner, disconnected from the images — the modality gap — and classifying by nearest word is barely above chance. As you raise the alignment, each name migrates into the middle of its own pictures and zero-shot accuracy climbs sharply — learned with no class labels, only which caption matched which image. (This is the flattened 2D view, so the numbers here read lower than the full-space 80% the why page measured — the shape of the story is the same.) Click a name and the images it would retrieve light up: the classifier is built out of words, so you can query it with any word you like. That is the whole open-vocabulary superpower, in one picture.
