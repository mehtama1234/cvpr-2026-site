Attention · the sandbox

Click a token — watch it choose what to look at

The why page says attention lets each token pick, by content, which other tokens to pull from. Here you can do it: click any word to make it the query and see how much it attends to each earlier word. Switch the head from meaning to position, and slide the temperature to sharpen or diffuse the lookup. Idealized (the word vectors are hand-set to illustrate the mechanism), but the operation — match, soften, weighted-average — is the real thing.

What to notice. (1) Query it on the meaning head: it attends most to cat — the model resolves what “it” refers to, purely by content, across the whole sentence. (2) Switch to the position head: every token just looks at the one right before it — that is the previous-token head, the first half of the induction circuit from the why page. (3) Turn temperature down: the lookup commits to a single token; turn it up: attention spreads out toward a flat average (and parks on the first token — the “sink”). Two heads, one soft dictionary lookup — that is a Transformer layer.
