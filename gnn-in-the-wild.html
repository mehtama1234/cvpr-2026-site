What changes between vision and a robot

axisCVPR 2026 (vision)Robotics 2025
What the graph isobjects and relations extracted from an imagethe robot's joints, the fleet's agents, the scene's objects
What it is forreasoning, recognition, structured predictioncontrol, coordination, planning over a live map
Built or givenusually extracted from perceptionoften given by physical structure (morphology, comms)
The outputa scene graph, a relation, a classan action, a plan, a coordinated maneuver
The through-line. A graph neural network learns by message passing — each node mixes in its neighbours — so it fits any data whose meaning is in its connections. In vision it reasons over scene graphs it extracts; in robotics the robot, its fleet, and its scene ARE graphs it inhabits and acts over — the same primitive, a graph to reason about versus a graph to act in.
