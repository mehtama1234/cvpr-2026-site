The through-line. A neural ODE makes depth continuous: the hidden state follows a learned ODE, a smooth flow. Its continuous-normalizing-flow form is the exact transport ODE behind flow matching, so this small, elegant theme is really the mathematical parent of a mainstream one — the same primitive, a continuous flow you classify with versus a continuous flow you generate with.
