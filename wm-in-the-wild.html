A learned simulator — world models across CVPR & Robotics

The why page argued it from first principles: learn a network that predicts what happens next if you act, then dream futures and plan inside them — powerful, but haunted by compounding drift. This page shows where world models actually appear: as driving simulators and video engines you steer in vision, and as a robot's imagination it learns and plans inside.

What changes between vision and a robot

axisCVPR 2026 (vision)Robotics 2025
What the model is fora prettier, steerable simulation to look at or generatea cheap place to practice and plan before acting for real
What it predictsfuture frames / occupancy / latent scene statefuture states under the robot's own actions
The standing costlong-horizon drift and consistencydrift is a safety risk — kept short-horizon, corrected with privileged info
The payoffcontrollable generation, driving sim, datasample efficiency: learn in imagination, act in reality
The through-line. A world model is a learned simulator: predict what happens next, then dream and plan inside it. In vision it becomes a steerable video/occupancy simulator; in robotics it becomes the imagination a policy trains and plans inside — the same primitive, one to watch and one to act in.
