Decision math

CVPR Theme Portfolio Map is not just a screen of labels. It is a measurement problem: the page is trying to decide whether a claim about the conference system should be trusted under pressure. The page summary says: Theme-to-system map for CVPR demos, production benches, Colab Pro+ evidence, cached-system evidence, and release gates.

Hidden quantity

The quantity underneath this page is covered mass: how much of the real conference, theme, system, or failure space is actually represented by the rows on the page.

Evidence

Counts are evidence only after the denominator is known. Covered items, missing items, duplicates, and untested branches all matter because coverage is a fraction, not a raw number.

Decision rule

The mathematical move is set accounting: define the universe, mark which elements have evidence, and inspect the uncovered complement instead of celebrating the covered set alone.

Failure test

Coverage fails when the page counts easy-to-find items while the missing region contains the actual risk. A high numerator means little if the denominator was chosen after looking.

Fundamental principles at play: sets and embeddings · information theory · uncertainty. These are the same mathematical ideas used in the math pages, but here they are attached to operational decisions: what is measured, how uncertain the measurement is, and what action the evidence permits.
