What changes between vision and a robot

axisCVPR 2026 (vision)Robotics 2025
The shiftcorruption, new domain, new video or viewpointnew weather, terrain, sensor, or the sim-to-real gap
The signalentropy, batch-norm stats, prototypes, promptsself-supervision, meta-learning, tactile feedback
When it runsat inference on the test batchonline, continuously, on the robot
The riskover-adapting collapses the modela wrong online update becomes a wrong action
The through-line. Test-time adaptation keeps a trained model learning after deployment, from the unlabelled test stream, when the world has shifted. In vision it recalibrates or prompt-tunes a frozen model per input; on a robot it adapts control and perception online as conditions drift — the same primitive, adapting a prediction versus adapting an action, and both can over-adapt and fail.
