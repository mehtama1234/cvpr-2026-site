The through-line. A normalizing flow reshapes a Gaussian into the data with an invertible map, so it gives the exact probability of any point. In vision that makes it the tool for density estimation, reversible transforms, and anomaly detection; on a robot the same exact density becomes a real-time normality score — the same primitive, a likelihood to compare versus an alarm to raise. And in the continuous limit it is the ancestor of flow matching.
