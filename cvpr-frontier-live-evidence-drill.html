Decision math

Frontier Live Evidence Drill is not just a screen of labels. It is a measurement problem: the page is trying to decide whether a claim about the conference system should be trusted under pressure. The page summary says: Live-backed evidence drill for the first promoted repo wave: five frontier sensor-fusion repos, their promoted Colab result rows, evidence artifacts, failure probes, and replay commands.

Hidden quantity

The quantity underneath this page is counterfactual behavior: what the system would do if the scene, input, user goal, timing, or failure condition changed in a specific way.

Evidence

A scenario is evidence when it states the changed condition and shows the resulting decision. The comparison is between the original world and the changed world.

Decision rule

The rule is causal: change one cause, measure the effect, and reject explanations that cannot predict the direction of the change.

Failure test

Scenario testing fails when every scenario is a renamed happy path. It needs uncomfortable changes that distinguish a robust rule from memorized behavior.

Fundamental principles at play: q-learning · kalman filtering · uncertainty. These are the same mathematical ideas used in the math pages, but here they are attached to operational decisions: what is measured, how uncertain the measurement is, and what action the evidence permits.
