PSNR = reconstruction quality (higher is better)
What to notice. Drag the budget from a handful of blobs to a few hundred and the scene snaps into focus — then stops improving, because this simple picture only holds so much detail (real splatting adds blobs only where they are needed). Now flip the shape to round: at the same budget the edges go mushy and the score drops, because a round blob cannot lie along an edge. Let the blobs stretch again and the edges come back. One long thin blob does the work of a dozen round ones — the whole reason real 3D Gaussian splatting lets its Gaussians stretch.
