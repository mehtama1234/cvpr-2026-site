What the shape shows. The dense knot in the middle is the softmax / matching / collapse cluster — attention, mixture-of-experts, CLIP, DETR, object slots — the families that all lean on turning a hard choice into a soft, differentiable one and then fighting its degenerate solution. The generative themes (diffusion, flow matching, autoregression) hang together off one side; the geometry themes (NeRF, splatting, depth, flow) off another, bound by differentiable rendering and underdetermination. The reinforcement and adversarial nodes bridge to the rest through the shared gradient-of-an-expectation trick. The clusters are not imposed — they fall out of which mathematics each theme reuses.
